Why Institutions Bring Us In

Most lending operations don't have a technology problem. They have a workflow orchestration problem.

Stalled LOS Implementations

Abrigo, nCino, or in-house systems that went live but never delivered the promised efficiency gains. Manual workarounds quietly multiplied.

Fragmented Integrations

Jack Henry, Symitar, or SilverLake stitched to your LOS with brittle middleware. Data flows in three directions and reconciles in none.

AI Pressure Without a Plan

The board wants an AI story. Vendors are pitching point solutions. Nobody has connected AI to an actual operational workflow.

Modernization Without Direction

You know the next 24 months matter. You don't have a roadmap that ties technology, workflow, and governance into a single executable plan.

What You Get

Five workstreams. One integrated roadmap. Built for the executive committee.

01

Workflow Diagnostic

End-to-end review of your lending pipeline — origination through booking. We surface the bottlenecks, manual handoffs, and decision-point gaps that no system report will show you.

02

LOS Optimization Analysis

Deep-dive review of how your Abrigo / Sageworks (or alternative LOS) is actually being used versus how it was designed. Decision models, document automation, eSign, exception handling.

03

Integration Audit

Map your core, LOS, document management, and ancillary platforms. Identify breakage points, data quality issues, and middleware risk before it becomes an outage.

04

AI Readiness Evaluation

Honest assessment of where AI delivers operational lift in your environment — underwriting, document intelligence, exception management — and where it doesn't (yet).

05

Executive Roadmap

A sequenced, prioritized 12–24 month modernization plan with clear owners, dependencies, and investment requirements. Built to present to your board and execute against.

Who This Is For

Designed for executives who need clarity, not a sales cycle.

This is the right fit if…

  • You're a community bank or credit union, $500M–$10B in assets
  • You're running Abrigo, Sageworks, or evaluating an LOS change
  • You're integrated with (or migrating between) Jack Henry, Symitar, or SilverLake
  • You've started — or stalled — a modernization initiative

This isn't the right fit if…

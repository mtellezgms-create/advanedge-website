Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Advanedge LLC ("Advanedge Consulting," "we," "our," or "us") operates the website advanedgeconsulting.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you provide directly. When you contact us by email or book a call through our scheduling link, you may provide your name, email address, job title, and institution name.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our Site may collect standard server log data including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URLs. This data is used for security and operational purposes only.

We do not operate any contact forms or collect payment information directly on this Site.

2. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Calendly — used for scheduling discovery calls. When you book a call, Calendly collects your name, email, and any details you enter. See Calendly's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google's servers. Google may log requests. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — this Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server log data. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

3. How We Use Information

We use information collected through this Site to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule discovery calls
  • Communicate about advisory engagements
  • Maintain and improve the Site

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies

This Site does not set first-party cookies. Third-party services (Calendly, Google Fonts) may set their own cookies as described in their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact information only for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or fulfill an advisory engagement. Email correspondence is retained in accordance with standard business record-keeping practices.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at info@advanedgeconsulting.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

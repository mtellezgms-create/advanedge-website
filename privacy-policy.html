Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Advanedge Consulting ("we," "us," or "our") operates advanedgeconsulting.com. This page explains how we handle information collected when you visit our website or interact with us.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you contact us, book a call, or submit an inquiry. This may include your name, email address, job title, and institution name.

We also collect standard web analytics data automatically — including pages visited, time on site, browser type, and general geographic location — through tools like Google Analytics. This data is aggregated and not linked to individual identities.

How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used solely to respond to your inquiry, schedule discovery calls, and communicate about our services. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Analytics data helps us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve the content and experience.

Scheduling and Third-Party Tools

Our website links to Calendly for scheduling discovery calls. When you book a call, you are subject to Calendly's own privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policy at calendly.com.

Cookies

Our website uses standard browser cookies to support analytics and improve site performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Some features of the site may not function as intended if cookies are disabled.

Email Communication

If you contact us by email, we retain that correspondence to respond to your inquiry and maintain records of our interaction. You may request deletion of your correspondence at any time by contacting us directly.

Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and served over HTTPS. However, no method of internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete any personal information you have provided to us at any time. To make a request, contact us at the email address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at info@advanedgeconsulting.com.
